# SCP：常态的代价 | SCP: Necessary Measures

> 一款以 SCP 基金会最高层战略决策、组织治理与长期后果为核心的单人管理游戏。
>
> A single-player management game about strategic decision-making, institutional governance, and long-term consequences at the highest level of the SCP Foundation.

## 项目概览 | Overview

《SCP：常态的代价》是一款正在开发中的 SCP 主题战略管理游戏。玩家将在相互分歧的时间线中承担不同身份与责任，在有限信息、组织约束和不断升级的异常威胁下作出决策。项目重点不是让最高决策层处理每一项日常事务，而是表现战略授权、机构制衡、危机升级、议会决策及其长期代价。

*SCP: Necessary Measures* is an SCP-themed strategy and management game currently in development. Across diverging timelines, players assume different identities and responsibilities, making decisions under incomplete information, institutional constraints, and escalating anomalous threats. The project focuses on strategic delegation, institutional checks and balances, crisis escalation, council decisions, and their long-term consequences rather than turning the highest authority into a routine approval system.
